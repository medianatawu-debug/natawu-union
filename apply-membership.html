Authorisation for Deduction of Trade Union Subscription

In terms of Section 13 of the Labour Relations Act, 66 of 1995, I, the undersigned member, hereby authorise my employer named in this application to deduct my trade union membership subscription/levies from my salary or wages on a monthly basis, and to pay such amount over to the National African Transport & Allied Workers Union (NATAWU) on my behalf.

I understand that the subscription amount will be determined from time to time by NATAWU in accordance with its constitution, and that my employer will act on written notice from NATAWU confirming the applicable amount.

This authorisation shall remain valid and binding until I cancel it in writing, in accordance with Section 13(4) of the Labour Relations Act, by giving my employer and NATAWU one (1) calendar month's written notice of cancellation. This authorisation may not be cancelled during any period in which I am subject to a collective agreement that requires this deduction as a condition of employment, where applicable.

I authorise my employer to disclose to NATAWU such information relating to my employment as is reasonably required by NATAWU to administer my membership, including but not limited to my salary, employee number, department, and position.

By ticking "I agree" below, I confirm that I have read and understood this authorisation and that I am agreeing to it voluntarily as my electronic signature.

Section 5 of 6

Terms, Conditions & Consent

Please read the terms below in full. Scroll to the end to unlock the confirmation checkbox.

Draft wording — verify against original form

1. Membership

Membership of NATAWU is open to all workers within the sectors it represents. Membership is subject to the union's constitution, as amended from time to time, and to payment of the applicable subscription.

2. Subscriptions

Members agree to pay the monthly subscription determined by NATAWU, either by stop order deduction as authorised in Section 3 of this application, or by direct payment where a stop order is not available.

3. Representation

NATAWU will represent members in matters relating to their employment in accordance with the union's constitution, applicable collective agreements, and the Labour Relations Act, 66 of 1995.

4. Resignation

A member may resign from NATAWU by giving written notice in accordance with the union's constitution. Resignation does not relieve a member of subscription obligations that arose prior to the effective date of resignation.

5. Accuracy of Information

The applicant warrants that all information provided in this application is true, complete and correct, and undertakes to inform NATAWU promptly of any change to these details.

6. Protection of Personal Information (POPIA)

NATAWU collects the personal information in this application — including identity, contact, employment, salary and next-of-kin information — solely for the purposes of processing this membership application, administering membership, and representing the member's interests as required under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

This information will be shared only with the applicant's employer (for stop order purposes), NATAWU officials and administrators directly involved in processing this application, and as otherwise required by law. NATAWU will take reasonable technical and organisational measures to keep this information secure and will not sell or disclose it to unrelated third parties.

The applicant has the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information held by NATAWU, subject to the union's record-keeping obligations under applicable labour legislation.

7. Consent

By submitting this application, the applicant consents to NATAWU processing their personal information as described above, and confirms that they have read and understood these terms and conditions in full.
