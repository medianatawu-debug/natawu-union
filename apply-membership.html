Before You Start

Choose whichever is easier for you — both paths end at the same Submit button below.

A Complete by Hand

  1. 1Download the form below.
  2. 2Print and complete it in CAPITAL LETTERS using BLACK ink, then sign it.
  3. 3Take a clear photo (or scan) of every completed page — make sure the writing is easy to read.

B Complete Electronically

  1. 1Download the same form below.
  2. 2Type your details directly into it on your computer or phone — every field and box is fillable, no printing needed.
  3. 3Sign it on-screen using Fill & Sign in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) or Preview's Markup tool on Mac/iPhone, then save it.
